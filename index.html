Privacy Policy for Impact Shopping List

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Impact Shopping List is a personal shopping-list application for Android developed by Impact Group. This Privacy Policy explains how Impact Shopping List handles information when you use the app.

Information You Enter

Impact Shopping List lets you create and manage information such as:

  • shopping lists and the items on them (names, quantities, notes and prices)
  • stores, including stores you search for and save
  • saved products (name, brand, size, barcode number and product picture)
  • templates and your shopping history, including the totals of finished shopping trips
  • receipts, including photos of receipts and the items and amounts read from them
  • photos you attach to items or stores
  • app settings such as language, region, reminders and your online-feature choices

Impact Shopping List does not require you to create an account.

Local Data Storage

The information above is stored on your device, in the app's private storage. Impact Group does not operate a server that receives or stores your shopping lists, shopping history, receipts or photos. The only information the app sends to an Impact Group server is what you choose to send, as described under "Contact and Support Messages" and "Donations" below.

You can remove this information by deleting records inside the app (for example lists, stores, saved products and the shopping history), by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Backup and Restore

The app can create a backup file of your data when you ask it to, and restore from such a file later. The file is saved or shared where you choose, and you are responsible for keeping it safe. Impact Group does not receive it. A backup file does not contain the keys used for device sync.

Camera, Photos and Receipt Scanning

The app asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo, for example of a receipt or of an item. When you pick a photo from your gallery, the app uses the Android picker and copies the photo you selected into the app's private storage. The app does not scan your photo library in the background.

Text on a receipt is read on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition, whose recognition models are bundled in the app. Your receipt photos are not sent to Impact Group or to a recognition server. Like other Google ML Kit features, the ML Kit library may send diagnostic and usage information to Google (see "Third-Party Services" below).

Optional Online Lookups

Online product search and online store lookup are two separate switches in the app, and both are off until you turn them on. When a switch is on, the app connects to the internet for that feature in these cases:

  • Product search (when you search for a product or open its details) sends the words you typed, or a product's barcode number, and your language to Open Food Facts, and downloads product pictures from its servers.
  • Store search (when you search for a store) sends the words you typed, your language and the country code of your region to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.
  • Store logos: for saved stores that belong to a well-known brand, the app also downloads the brand's logo in the background from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, using the brand's public Wikidata identifier. These requests do not contain your lists, history or receipts.

These services receive your device's IP address, like any internet request, and handle information under their own privacy policies. The app does not use your GPS location and does not read your contacts. Impact Group does not receive these requests. If you leave a switch off, the app makes none of the connections for that feature.

Contact and Support Messages

The Support screen has a Contact form. Sending a message is optional. When you tap Send, the app sends the following to the Impact Group support server over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • the e-mail address you typed, so that we can reply (the address is not verified);
  • the subject, the category and the text of your message; and
  • technical details: the app's language, the app version, the platform (Android), the time, and a random identifier of the message.

The support server is hosted on Vercel. It checks the message, limits repeated requests, and passes the message by e-mail, through the e-mail delivery service MailerSend, to the developer's mailbox. The server does not write messages to a database or to its logs. To recognise an accidental double submission it keeps the request in its working memory for about ten minutes, and for about a minute it counts requests per network address to limit abuse. Vercel and MailerSend process the message as service providers under their own privacy policies; MailerSend may keep delivery records under its policy.

We use your e-mail address and message only to read and answer your message. The message stays in the developer's mailbox until it is deleted (see "Data Retention and Deletion"). Please do not put sensitive information, such as passwords or payment details, in a message.

Donations

The Support screen has an optional Donate tab. Donating is voluntary, the money goes directly to the developer, and donating does not unlock or change anything in the app.

  • PayPal: the app opens the developer's PayPal.me page (paypal.me/impactpsyk) in your browser or in the PayPal app, with the amount you chose. PayPal handles the payment under its own privacy policy.
  • PromptPay: the app shows a PromptPay QR code, created on your device, that holds the developer's PromptPay receiver ID and the amount you chose. You scan it in your own banking app, and your bank handles the payment.

The app does not receive or store your payment details, and it cannot see whether you paid. After you have paid, you can tap "Yes, I've donated" to let the developer know. The app then sends the Impact Group support server the payment method, the amount and currency, the optional note you typed, the app version, the platform, the time and a random identifier. No name, e-mail address or account information is included. This notice is handled like a contact message (see above), and tapping the button is not proof of payment.

Device-to-Device Sync

Impact Shopping List includes an optional sync feature that transfers your lists, items, stores and shopping history directly between devices that you have paired and that can reach each other on the same local network. It runs only while you have turned it on.

To pair two devices, one device shows a pairing code (as a QR code) that contains a device identifier, a public key, a device name and the device's local network address, and you enter that code on the other device. The data is encrypted by the app before it is sent, using keys created on your devices when you pair them; the keys are kept in the app's secure storage on your device. The sync does not go through a server operated by Impact Group.

Notifications

If you turn on shopping reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. Reminders are created and shown locally; the app does not use a push-notification server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Impact Budget Hand-Off

If you choose to send a shopping total to Impact Budget (another Impact Group app) and it is installed on the same device, the app sends the details of that shopping trip - store name, purchase date and time, currency, totals and the list of items with quantities and prices - directly to Impact Budget on your device after you pick a wallet and confirm. This transfer happens between the two apps on your device; it does not use the internet or a server operated by Impact Group. Nothing is sent unless you confirm.

Advertising

The free version of Impact Shopping List displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • app and user interactions
  • diagnostic information
  • device and account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Impact Shopping List does not give advertisers your shopping lists, shopping history, receipts or photos. For information about Google's data practices, please review Google's privacy policies and information relating to Google AdMob.

Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Impact Shopping List uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required. Depending on your location and applicable law, Google may show a consent or privacy message before advertising requests are permitted. Where required, the app also gives you access to Google's privacy-options screen so that you can review or change your advertising choices. Your advertising choice is separate from the choice about online product and store lookups.

Third-Party Services

Impact Shopping List currently integrates with the following services:

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent)
  • Google ML Kit text recognition (on-device receipt scanning). According to Google, ML Kit may collect device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app information (package name and version), per-installation identifiers that are not intended to uniquely identify a user or device, and performance and diagnostic information, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties.
  • Open Food Facts, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons (only for the online lookups you turn on)
  • Vercel (hosts the Impact Group support server) and MailerSend (delivers e-mail), only for the messages and donation notices you choose to send
  • PayPal, and your own bank through PromptPay, only if you choose to donate; these payments are handled outside the app
  • Google Play, which distributes the app and collects its own statistics, such as installs and crash reports, under Google's privacy policy

These services process information under their own privacy policies. Impact Group does not control data that Google or the other providers collect independently.

Data Sharing

Impact Group does not sell your personal information, and your shopping lists, history, receipts and photos are not uploaded to an Impact Group server. Information may nevertheless be transmitted:

  • to Google, as part of advertising, consent management and ML Kit diagnostics, as described above;
  • to Open Food Facts, OpenStreetMap or Wikimedia, for the online lookups you turn on;
  • to the Impact Group support server, and through it to its hosting and e-mail providers, when you send a message or a donation notice;
  • to PayPal or to your bank, when you choose to donate;
  • to another device you have paired, when you use device sync; or
  • to Impact Budget on your device, when you choose to send a shopping total.

Data Security

Your information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Connections that the app makes to the internet use HTTPS, including messages to the support server. Device sync data is encrypted by the app. We recommend that you protect your device with a screen lock.

Data Retention and Deletion

Impact Shopping List does not require an account, so there is no account to delete. You can delete locally stored information by deleting records inside the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

A message or donation notice that you send reaches the developer by e-mail and stays in the developer's mailbox until it is deleted; the support server does not store it beyond the few minutes in working memory described above. You can ask us to delete a message you sent by writing to the e-mail address below. If you need help with other data associated with Impact Shopping List, contact us at the same address.

Children's Privacy

Impact Shopping List is intended for users aged 18 and over. The app is not directed to children.
